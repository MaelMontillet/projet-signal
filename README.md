In this repository you can find the notebook of one of the main project of semester 2 of master 1 of ModIA (ProjetFilRouge.ipynb).
It is audio recongnition with preprocessing with Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MCC) and machine learning (multinomial regression, random forests, SVM, ...).
Thanks to the concatenation of two statistics instead of one : the mean and standard deviation along time of the MCC, we achieves the best classification of among all the groups of the class.
